## Esri
I worked as a content developer to write weekly technical articles for their support knowledge base. We look through the support cases from their support backend, and identify issues faced by customers that have been resolved by our support staff to become topics for our articles. This would then create a knowledge base so that future similar cases that our support staff would encounter would be able to reference customers to directly, and reduce the overall calls coming in to our support.

Our articles are researched, reproduced which required trained familiarity with Esri products, written in certain templates for standardized style, tested by technical editors, and verified by a copy editor before publishing. I eventually transitioned to a copy editor from my content developer role, gatekeeping and ensuring that articles written by our content developers abide by our writing standards, and have passed all checks before being published on the support knowledge base.

## Digital Route
In Digital Route, I was an embedded technical writer in several development teams, documenting whatever documentation changes that is required as they develop issues in their respective swimlanes. Adopting the scrum methodology of software development, I attended daily standups with the development teams, tracked ongoing development issues, identified if there were any documentation requirements to be made, communicated with developers and other relevant stakeholders (this can vary from the product owner to customer-facing implementors) to review and verify the documentation, and overseeing the publication of documentation to its latest state upon release.

Since most of my time there was documentation maintenance, it is quite difficult to showcase my documentation work done during my time at Digital Route for their range of Usage products ([MediationZone](https://infozone.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/MD95/overview), [Usage Engine Private Edition](https://infozone.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/UEPE5D/overview), [UsageCloud](https://infozone.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DAZ/overview).
